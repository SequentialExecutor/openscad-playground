# First-part tutorial

Open [first-part.forma3d](first-part.forma3d) to explore the part built in the
[user guide](../../USER_GUIDE.md#make-your-first-part-a-plate-with-a-hole): a
60 × 40 × 6 mm plate resting at Z=0, with an 8 mm centered through-hole. Its box,
cylindrical cutter and Difference remain editable. The documentation capture
script creates this native project through the actual UI and verifies the
exported STL bounds and hole volume.

# Editable example: rotated block

Open `rotated-block.forma3d.json` using the visual workspace's Open project.
The centered box has local dimensions 50 × 30 × 20 mm, position (15, 0, 10) mm
and a 90° Z rotation. Its world bounds are (0, −25, 0) to (30, 25, 20) mm.

`rotated-block.stl` was generated by the actual OpenSCAD/Manifold browser worker
in the automated visual editor test. Its binary STL vertex bounds were checked
against those values. The file has 12 triangles; it was not substituted with a
Three.js preview. The editable JSON contains a stable generated body ID.

# Editable boolean hole

Open `editable-hole.forma3d`: a 50 mm cube with a 10 mm diameter through-hole.
The cylinder and cube remain separate editable operands of a difference feature.
Change Cylinder's Diameter to 15 mm, then undo to restore 10 mm.
`editable-hole.stl` is the actual WASM/Manifold export. Tests inspect its bounds,
inner radius, volume and void through the center rather than only file existence.

# Sketch plate with a dependent hole

`sketch-plate.forma3d` contains a constrained 60 × 30 mm rectangle extruded 12 mm,
with a 6 mm through-hole at (10, 10) mm. Edit Sketch extrusion's profile width:
the referenced Difference feature recomputes while preserving both operands.
The test changed width from 40 to 60 mm, undid/redid it, reloaded the project and
checked the actual exported volume and bounds. `sketch-plate.stl` is that kernel
export, not the viewport mesh.


# Parametric enclosure

`parametric-enclosure.forma3d` is a 120 × 60 × 30 mm open enclosure with 3 mm
walls/floor, 5 mm corner radius, four 8 mm posts (6 mm above the floor) and 3 mm
through-holes. Their 10 mm mounting inset follows width/depth edits. The browser
test changed width from 100 to 120 mm, checked new/old hole locations and the
floor/post heights in exact triangles, then saved and reloaded the project.
`parametric-enclosure.stl` is that actual OpenSCAD/Manifold export. The template
specification, transforms, IDs and printer settings remain editable in the native
project; mesh import is not required.


# Imported mesh with editable through-hole

`source-cube.stl` is a known 10 × 10 × 10 mm source mesh. Import it with mm units
and plate placement, or open `imported-mesh.forma3d`. The schema-5/native-v2 project
contains the original normalized triangle asset, an imported body centered on the
plate, a 4 mm cylindrical cutter and their editable Difference feature. The adjacent
`imported-mesh.stl` is exported by the actual browser OpenSCAD/Manifold worker via
the documentation capture script. The source mesh is retained as mesh geometry;
this does not reconstruct a sketch or parametric cube from triangles.
